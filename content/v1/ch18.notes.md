## Volume I, Chapter 18 (checked against marxists.org, 2026-10-20)

- Verified in the text: formula I (s/v = surplus-value/value of labour-power = surplus-labour/
  necessary labour); the classical "derivative formulae" II (surplus-labour/working day,
  surplus-value/value of the product, surplus-product/total product) "falsely" express the degree
  of exploitation; with a 12-hour day split 6 and 6 and 3 shillings each, 100% versus 50%; under
  formula II surplus "can never reach 100%"; Lavergne's estimate that the English agricultural
  labourer gets 1/4, so 300%; the "false semblance of an association"; formula III, unpaid
  labour/paid labour, as "only a popular expression"; the capitalist pays the value of
  labour-power; "Capital ... is essentially the command over unpaid labour"; profit, interest and
  rent as forms of unpaid labour.
- Moore and Aveling title: "Various Formula for the Rate of Surplus-Value". Fowkes title as in
  lib/kapital.ts ("Different Formulae ...") is from memory; please check.
