## Volume I, Chapter 6 (checked against marxists.org, 2026-10-20)

- Verified in the text: labour-power as "the aggregate of those mental and physical capabilities";
  sold "only for a definite period", otherwise he would turn "from a free man into a slave"; the
  "free labourer, free in the double sense"; "Nature does not produce on the one side owners of
  money ... and on the other men possessing nothing but their own labour-power" (a historical
  result); children ("his substitutes") and education or training enter the value; "a historical
  and moral element"; the labourer "everywhere gives credit to the capitalist"; "a very Eden of the
  innate rights of man ... Freedom, Equality, Property and Bentham"; "the hidden abode of
  production" and the sign "No admittance except on business".
- Site choices: the 3-coin day and its split (food 1.2, roof 0.6, clothes and firewood 0.4,
  children 0.5, normal comforts 0.3) are invented to illustrate; only the 3-coin total comes from
  CONTINUITY.md (Marx's 3 shillings). Marx's closing image of the worker bringing "his own hide to
  market" with nothing to expect but "a hiding" was softened to "Sam follows slowly, holding back"
  for young readers.
- Bentham is glossed as "a thinker who said people follow their own interest". That is the sense
  Marx uses him in; Bentham's utilitarianism is broader (the greatest happiness of the greatest
  number). Consider rewording.
- Critics (my summaries): marginal-product wage theory; Robert Nozick on free contracts.
