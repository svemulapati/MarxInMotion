# Vol I, Ch 13 notes (checked against marxists.org ch13.htm, 2026-10-20)

- Verified: a dozen masons passing stones from the foot of a ladder to its summit; the collective
  working day of 144 hours making "far more" than twelve isolated men; raising a heavy weight; the
  cavalry squadron vs individual soldiers; social contact stimulating "animal spirits"; "a single
  violin player is his own conductor; an orchestra requires a separate one"; managers and
  overlookers as officers and sergeants; the capitalist pays "the value of 100 independent
  labour-powers, but he does not pay for the combined labour-power"; "this power costs capital
  nothing" and appears as a power capital has by nature; resistance grows with numbers; control is
  "despotic" in form; co-operation is "the fundamental form of the capitalist mode of production".
- Site choices: the violinist is drawn as "she" (Marx says "his"); captions say "their own
  conductor". The new-workshop building and Sam's ladder idea are invented. The log-lifting scene
  stands in for Marx's "raising a heavy weight".
- Critics: Coase (1937), Alchian and Demsetz (1972). My summaries.
