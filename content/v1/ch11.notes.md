## Volume I, Chapter 11 (checked against marxists.org, 2026-10-20)

- Verified in the text: mass of surplus-value = variable capital advanced × rate of surplus-value;
  the 500 labourers / 1,500s. against 100 labourers at 200% or an 18-hour day / 600s., never
  reaching 1,200s. (24 hours); "contradicts all experience based on appearance" with the cotton
  spinner and the baker; the minimum sum: a worker needing 8 hours, 4 hours of surplus labour,
  two labourers to live like a labourer, "8 times" the minimum capital to live twice as well and
  turn half the surplus into capital; the "small master"; medieval guilds limiting labourers per
  master; Hegel's quantity into quality; "capital acquired the command over labour"; "a coercive
  relation"; "no longer the labourer that employs the means of production, but the means of
  production that employ the labourer".
- Animation 11.3 reads Marx's "8 times" as 8 helpers (8 × the cost of one labour-power): 2 helpers
  give 8 surplus hours (one living); twice as well needs 16; saving half doubles it to 32 hours,
  which is 8 helpers at 4 hours each. Please double-check this reading.
