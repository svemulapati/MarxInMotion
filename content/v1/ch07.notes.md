## Volume I, Chapter 7 (checked against marxists.org, 2026-10-20)

- Verified in the text: the three elementary factors of the labour process (work, its subject,
  its instruments); the bee and "the worst architect"; the two features of the labour process under
  capital (work under the capitalist's control; the product is his property); 10 lb of cotton for
  10s, 2s of spindle wear, 3s for a day's labour-power, a 6-hour day giving 15s yarn for 15s
  advanced ("Our capitalist stares in astonishment"); the excuses (abstinence, "out of nothing",
  his own superintendence) ending with the capitalist's "hearty laugh"; the 12-hour day where 27s
  become 30s; "The trick has at last succeeded".
- Title: Fowkes "The Labour Process and the Valorization Process"; Moore/Aveling "The
  Labour-Process and the Process of Producing Surplus-Value". The glossary entry for valorization
  mentions the Moore/Aveling wording.
- Critics are my summaries: J. B. Clark's marginal productivity; Nozick's voluntary-exchange
  objection (Anarchy, State, and Utopia, ch. 8). Worth a fairness read.
